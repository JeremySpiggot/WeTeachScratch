Why Scratch

Visual coding that keeps learning fun and creative.

Scratch turns coding into a creative playground. Students can see their ideas come alive immediately, which makes it perfect for learners who are curious, artistic, or building games for the first time.

This platform blends a structured course flow with project-based learning so students aren't just watching tutorials — they're making their own games and solving their own problems.

What learners will build

  • Platformers and side-scrolling adventures
  • Story-driven interactive scenes
  • Custom characters and animated effects
  • Logic puzzles and game systems
  • Original projects from beginner to advanced level
